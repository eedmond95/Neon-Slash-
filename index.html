HOW TO PLAY

Tap a target for a precise hit, or drag your finger through groups. Avoid screen edges on iPhone. A 12-hit combo triggers Fever, and late slashes earn Perfect Cut bonuses.

◇ PINK CUBESSlash for points and combos.
▣ BLUE ARMORBreak its armor with repeated hits.
● GREEN ORBSRestore health or energy.
✹ RED BOMBSAvoid touching them with your blade.

New threats: gold prisms grant energy, orange splitters create two more cubes, violet phasers dodge, and orange chargers rush. Lyra Pulse clears incoming hazards—including bombs—for its full animation.

Boss protocol: build Overdrive and use PULSE to break the shield. Slash the exposed core. Slash the glowing EMP node before its countdown reaches zero to avoid a direct hit. Try Boss Rush for four commanders. Press Space on desktop.
