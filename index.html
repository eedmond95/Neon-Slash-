HOW TO PLAY

Tap a target for a precise hit, or drag your finger through groups. Avoid screen edges on iPhone. A 12-hit combo triggers Fever, and late slashes earn Perfect Cut bonuses.

◇ PINK CUBESSlash for points and combos.
▣ BLUE ARMORBreak its armor with repeated hits.
● GREEN ORBSRestore health or energy.
✹ RED BOMBSAvoid touching them with your blade.

New threats: cyan Echo Drones detonate nearby enemies when slashed, gold prisms grant energy, orange splitters create two more cubes, violet phasers dodge, and orange chargers rush. Lyra Pulse clears incoming hazards—including bombs—for its full animation.

Boss protocol: build Overdrive and use PULSE to break the shield. Slash the exposed core. Slash the orange EMP node to disarm it. When a boss reaches half health, it enters Rage Protocol: cut the RED laser reactor twice before it fires. Try Boss Rush for four commanders. Press Space on desktop.
